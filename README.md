### 🔬 About Me & Experimentation Journey

I am a **technology lover and curious experimenter**. Rather than following conventional boundaries, I love diving deep into how systems work, tearing down complex concepts, and testing software to its absolute limits:

- 🧪 **Hands-On Experimentation:** Constantly exploring new technologies, testing unconventional workflows, and finding simpler, faster ways to solve technical problems.
- ⚡ **Exploring High-Performance Tech:** Fascinated by raw speed, low-level concurrency in **Rust**, lightweight architectures, and zero-bloat software.
- 📱 **Android & Local Tech Tinkering:** Experimenting with native **Android**, local Wi-Fi networking, standalone tools, and offline-first setups that don't depend on the cloud.
- 💡 **Continuous Learning:** Always curious, always learning, and excited by the bleeding edge of modern technology.
