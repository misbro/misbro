### ⚡ About Me & Engineering Philosophy

I build software focused on **performance**, **stability**, and **zero-bloat execution**. Whether architecting multi-threaded system daemons or building native mobile client experiences, my focus is always on engineering quality:

- 🚀 **High Throughput & Low Latency:** Deep focus on asynchronous I/O, multi-threaded concurrency, socket reuse, and memory-safe systems engineering in **Rust**.
- 📱 **Robust Native Mobile Development:** Designing intuitive, battery-efficient, and offline-first mobile applications in **Kotlin** and **Java** with modern reactive architectures.
- 🔒 **Privacy-First & Zero-Cloud Reliance:** Prioritizing standalone architectures that work reliably offline without mandatory telemetry, cloud relays, or third-party tracking.
- 🛠️ **Clean Architecture & Maintainability:** Strict adherence to modular separation of concerns, defensive error handling, and crash-proof state journaling.
